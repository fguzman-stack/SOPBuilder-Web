Privacy policy
Política de privacidad

Effective: October 14, 2026. Controller: Francisco Guzmán, Santiago, Chile. Contact: familiazv2016@gmail.com.

1. Local application data / Datos locales

Your SOPs, execution records and attachments stay in your Windows user profile unless you explicitly export them or use an optional integration. SOPBuilder does not include telemetry or send this content to the activation service. The local SQLite database is not encrypted; app locking is not a substitute for Windows account security or disk encryption. Password-protected backups use AES-256-GCM.

Los SOPs, ejecuciones y adjuntos permanecen en tu perfil de Windows salvo que los exportes o uses una integración opcional. No se envían al servicio de activación. La base SQLite no está cifrada; el bloqueo de la app no sustituye la protección de Windows ni el cifrado del disco. Los respaldos con contraseña usan AES-256-GCM.

2. Website hosting / Alojamiento web

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not install advertising, analytics or third-party tracking scripts. A language preference may be stored locally in your browser; no advertising cookies are set by our website. GitHub processes connection information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. Clearing browser storage removes the language preference.

La web se aloja en GitHub Pages, sin scripts de publicidad, analítica ni rastreo de terceros. El idioma puede guardarse localmente en tu navegador. GitHub procesa información de conexión según su propia política.

3. License redemption / Canje de licencias

We use Supabase in the São Paulo region to store a SHA-256 hash of your code, batch reference, normalized email address, random license identifier, redemption time and revocation status. We need these to fulfill a license, recover it using the same code and email, prevent repeat redemption and handle refunds or abuse. We do not store the plaintext purchase code in the database. Your license file includes your email and must be kept private.

Usamos Supabase en la región de São Paulo para guardar la huella SHA-256 del código, lote, correo normalizado, identificador de licencia, fecha de canje y estado de revocación. Estos datos permiten entregar y recuperar la licencia, evitar duplicados y gestionar reembolsos o abuso. El archivo de licencia incluye tu correo y debes mantenerlo privado.

For abuse prevention, the service stores a salted hash of the connection IP and an attempt count. We do not deliberately log emails, codes or raw IP addresses in our function code, but infrastructure providers may maintain technical logs. A daily maintenance job removes rate-limit records older than 24 hours (up to 48 hours between runs). No email verification is performed: knowledge of the code is the possession proof, so do not share it.

4. Purposes, legal basis and retention / Finalidad y conservación

License fulfillment is necessary to perform the purchase agreement; security and fraud prevention are legitimate operational interests, where applicable law permits. License records are retained while needed to provide lifetime-license fulfillment and for applicable legal obligations; we do not promise an arbitrary deletion period that would prevent recovery. Support messages are retained only as long as needed for the request and applicable legal requirements. Ask us for access, correction, deletion or export of your license/support records, or to object to processing where applicable. We may need to verify your identity; deletion of fulfillment records can prevent license recovery. We will explain any legal reason that prevents deletion.

El canje permite cumplir la compra y prevenir fraude. Conservamos los registros necesarios para la licencia y obligaciones legales. Puedes solicitar acceso, corrección, eliminación o exportación mediante el correo de soporte; podemos necesitar verificar tu identidad y la eliminación puede impedir recuperar la licencia.

5. Optional external services / Servicios opcionales

Remote AI receives only the text you explicitly authorize and uses your provider account. Whisper.cpp processes audio locally after its initial download. Google Drive receives the SOPs, metadata and attachments you approve for personal manual sync or backups; ordinary Drive sync is not end-to-end encrypted by SOPBuilder. These providers have their own terms and privacy policies. Public Google OAuth configuration and verification are still prerequisites, not a completed product certification.

La IA remota recibe el texto que autorizas y usa tu cuenta del proveedor. Whisper procesa audio localmente. Drive recibe el contenido de las operaciones que confirmas; la sincronización ordinaria no añade cifrado de extremo a extremo.

6. Transfers, rights and security / Derechos y seguridad

GitHub, Supabase and services you choose may process data internationally under their contractual and privacy safeguards. See Supabase privacy and the relevant service's policies. We restrict database access to the server and keep signing keys out of the public website. No system is guaranteed immune from breaches. If an incident affects your information, we will take appropriate steps and notify affected people or authorities when required by law. You can contact your competent data protection authority if you believe your rights were not respected.

7. Updates / Cambios

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Contact support before sending sensitive records, passwords, codes or license files.

Publicaremos los cambios importantes aquí con su fecha. No envíes documentos sensibles, contraseñas ni códigos en canales públicos.
